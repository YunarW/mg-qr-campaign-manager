# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Rebranded the plugin as **MG QR Campaign Manager** for reuse across multiple Muffin Graphics websites.
- Changed internal campaign post type and metadata prefixes to `mg_*`.
- Added automatic migration from legacy Hackortu QR campaign data.
- Kept configurable UTM parameters, short `/q/slug/` URLs, 302 redirects, campaign status, basic scan count, and latest-scan timestamp.
